CycleReady Privacy Policy

Effective: 31 August 2026

CycleReady is a personal adaptive cycling-coaching application operated by Neil Burns. This policy explains how CycleReady processes information when you connect the Android application, website, Supabase account or Intervals.icu account.

Information processed

CycleReady may process athlete profile information, cycling activities, power and heart-rate summaries, cadence, training load, planned workouts, goals, wellness information, sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, weight, subjective feedback, readiness, session analysis and coaching decisions. Connected services remain identifiable as the source of imported data.

Purpose

Information is used only to synchronize the athlete's records, analyse training and recovery, generate explainable coaching guidance, adapt the training plan, display the same information on authorized CycleReady clients, and maintain security and reliability.

Storage and sharing

Cloud records are stored in the CycleReady Supabase project and protected by authentication and row-level security. CycleReady does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Information is shared with a connected provider only when required to perform an action requested by the athlete, such as reading an Intervals.icu activity or publishing a planned workout.

Credentials

Provider API keys, OAuth secrets and privileged Supabase credentials are stored only in protected server-side secret storage. They are not included in the Android application or public website.

Retention and deletion

Records are retained while the athlete uses CycleReady or until deletion is requested. The athlete can disconnect a provider, revoke its authorization, export supported app data, or request deletion of CycleReady cloud records. Provider records held by Intervals.icu remain subject to the provider's own controls and privacy policy.

Security

CycleReady uses encrypted HTTPS connections, authenticated accounts, database access policies and auditable server-side processing. No online system can guarantee absolute security, but access is restricted to the minimum required for the coaching service.

Your choices

You may choose not to connect Intervals.icu, withdraw authorization through Intervals.icu settings, stop cloud synchronization, or request access, correction or deletion of CycleReady records.

Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests can be raised through the CycleReady project owner or the project's GitHub issue tracker. Do not include health information, passwords or API keys in a public issue.
